About Me

I am an AI student at École Polytechnique de Ouagadougou, passionate about applying technology to environmental challenges and sustainable development in Burkina Faso.

My journey has led me to develop diverse skills from Python programming to mobile development with Flutter, UX/UI design, and data analysis. I'm particularly interested in using AI to create impactful solutions for Burkina Faso's development.

One of my flagship projects, Smart-Apic, is an IoT-based beehive monitoring device using machine learning to improve local beekeeping production. This project, incubated at Orange Digital Center, reflects my vision of technology serving sustainable development.
